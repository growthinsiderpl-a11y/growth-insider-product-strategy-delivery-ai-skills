# Opportunity Assessment

Decide whether a product opportunity deserves more discovery,
immediate rejection, or bounded validation.

## When To Run It

- there is a candidate problem, request, or market signal
  competing for attention
- leaders want a crisp go, no-go, or not-yet recommendation
- evidence is mixed and the team risks arguing from intuition
- the team needs to compare an opportunity against other uses of
  capacity

## Do Not Run It When

- the team already committed to delivery and needs execution
  detail
- the request is really a campaign or channel idea owned by
  marketing
- the work is purely an engineering remediation with no product
  choice
- nobody will act differently based on the result

## Inputs

- opportunity statement
- segment and user context
- observed pain or workflow evidence
- current workaround
- strategic and delivery constraints
- existing metric signals

## Procedure

### Write the Opportunity Claim

- state the user, problem, and expected product value
- name the decision that will change
- remove feature language unless it is essential to the claim
- record what is still assumed rather than observed
- make the opportunity small enough to compare with alternatives

### Estimate Consequence

- describe the cost of the problem to the user or business
- identify affected segments and frequency
- capture downstream effects on retention, monetization, or
  expansion
- write what makes the opportunity urgent or safely deferrable
- distinguish local annoyance from systemic pain

### Check Evidence Quality

- separate direct user evidence from internal opinions
- mark missing quantitative or qualitative proof
- prefer recent behavioral evidence over old anecdotes
- log contradictions explicitly
- note if the current evidence reflects only one customer or one
  persona

### Compare Against Alternatives

- list at least one alternative use of the same capacity
- compare time to learning and reversibility
- surface hidden dependencies or enabling work
- do not assume the new request outranks existing bets by default
- state what must be deprioritized if this wins

### Recommend the Next Move

- choose reject, defer, validate, or commit to planning
- state why that path is minimum sufficient
- name the owner and due date
- define what evidence would upgrade or downgrade the opportunity
- capture the review trigger for revisiting the decision

## Decision Tests

- the opportunity is phrased as a problem worth solving, not a
  favorite feature
- evidence and assumptions are visibly separated
- the opportunity is compared against at least one plausible
  alternative
- the next step is smaller than full delivery unless evidence is
  already strong
- the recommendation explains what should not happen next

## Outputs

- one-page opportunity assessment
- explicit next-step recommendation
- evidence gap list
- comparison note against competing bets
- decision-ready summary for stakeholders

## Failure Modes

- endorsing the loudest request
- confusing stakeholder urgency with user importance
- pretending weak evidence is enough because the idea sounds
  strategic
- failing to name the opportunity cost

## Review and Follow-through

- review after the next evidence collection step
- retire the opportunity if the key assumption fails
- update the portfolio view if the opportunity is promoted
- store the rationale so later requests can be compared fairly

## Escalate or Stop When

- the opportunity depends on an unowned integration or policy
  change outside current control
- the evidence only exists in executive anecdotes and cannot be
  tested in the next cycle
- a different option solves the same user harm with lower
  reversibility risk
- stakeholders refuse to name what work would lose funding if this
  wins

## Worked Scenario

- A team debates whether to build approval delegation or custom
  dashboard theming.
- Opportunity assessment should rank delegation higher if support
  pain, expansion blockers, and governance risk are all present
  while theming is mostly sales theater.
- The output should still log what evidence would be required
  before theming returns as a serious candidate.
