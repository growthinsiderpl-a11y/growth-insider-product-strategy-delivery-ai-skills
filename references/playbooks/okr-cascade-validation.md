# OKR Cascade Validation

Check whether objectives, key results, and initiatives form a
coherent cascade rather than a stack of disconnected planning
artifacts.

## When To Run It

- quarterly planning is underway
- leaders suspect OKRs are performative
- initiatives have multiplied without clear outcome linkage
- teams need to know whether the cascade can guide
  prioritization

## Do Not Run It When

- the organization only needs an OKR template
- there are no objectives yet and the problem is strategy, not
  cascade quality
- teams want to use hidden scores to rank objectives
- the cascade is being treated as a substitute for product
  strategy

## Inputs

- objective list
- key results
- initiatives
- owners
- review cadence
- constraints

## Procedure

### Check Objective Quality

- confirm objectives describe meaningful change rather than
  tasks
- look for overlap or contradiction across objectives
- ensure time horizon and owner are explicit
- remove slogans that cannot guide choices
- note which objective lacks a real decision consequence

### Check Key Results

- verify each key result links to one objective
- ensure metrics are observable and time-bound
- flag key results that are really output counts
- look for missing baselines or targets
- surface key results that are too vague to steer prioritization

### Check Initiatives

- trace each initiative to a key result
- identify initiatives with no cascade link
- surface crowded objectives with too many weak initiatives
- check whether initiative mix matches the objective logic
- flag initiatives that are bigger than the time horizon or
  owner model can support

### Assess Governability

- review cadence, ownership, and review triggers
- confirm the cascade is usable in decision forums
- look for unowned measures or initiatives
- state where the cascade breaks
- show whether the cascade can actually reprioritize work

### Recommend Cleanup

- propose merges, deletions, relabeling, or new linkage
- preserve the smallest set of edits that restores coherence
- prepare a concise explanation for leadership
- define the next validation point
- carry structural issues into roadmap review if needed

## Decision Tests

- every key result belongs somewhere
- initiatives are not floating
- metrics are time-bound and observable
- objectives are decision-usable
- the cleanup list is concrete enough to execute

## Outputs

- OKR cascade review
- orphan initiative list
- missing-baseline list
- cleanup recommendation
- forum-readiness note

## Failure Modes

- accepting output counts as outcomes
- allowing initiatives to drive objectives retroactively
- keeping multiple overlapping objectives to satisfy politics
- treating cadence as governance when the cascade itself is
  broken

## Review and Follow-through

- revalidate after planning revisions
- use the result in roadmap and portfolio reviews
- retire cascade elements that no longer guide decisions
- capture which objective shifts require reweighting priorities

## Escalate or Stop When

- initiatives exist with no key-result path
- key results are outputs in disguise
- owners are missing from objectives or initiatives
- the cascade cannot explain why current roadmap items deserve
  space

## Worked Scenario

- An objective to improve expansion should not be supported by
  generic awareness tasks.
- It should connect to measurable key results such as governed-
  team conversion and the initiatives actually expected to move
  them.
- That is the level of specificity this validation should
  demand.
